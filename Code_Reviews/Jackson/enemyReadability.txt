EnemyManager is very readable and well organized. EnemyCreator is also well
organized and can be easily understood, but the bloated switch makes it less
readable than it could be.

The names are very clear and make it easy to understand what they are for.
All methods and variables in both files are well named and clear in their
purpose.

The switch does make the differences in enemies very clear though and is easy
to follow.

Some of the formatting is a little off in indentation and spacing, and some
of the methods are not capitalized where they could be, but there is nothing
egregious.
